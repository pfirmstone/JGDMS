Proxy host board layouts

Drawings of the rack case that runs untrusted smart proxies on separate hardware. Issue of 2026-11-23, revised midday. Twelve vertical cards, each carrying two SMARC i.MX 95 modules, load from the top into a backplane (RL-JGDMS-0215, -0217); each module has a network controller port of its own on six M.2 network modules, which stand on a passive carrier on the right-hand wall, each with its own MCIO cable to the relay board (RL-JGDMS-0223); the relay's outward NIC has an MCIO connector of its own, the board's OCP slot stays empty, and no MCIO cable carries SMBus (RL-JGDMS-0227, -0229). There are no activity LEDs (RL-JGDMS-0224). Cooling: RL-JGDMS-0225; module requirements: RL-JGDMS-0219; the controller, with no wiring to the relay side: RL-JGDMS-0226. K-numbers are items of the section "Case topology" in JGDMS/docs/relay-isolation/DESIGN-security.md; other letters and numbers are from PROXY_HOST_BOARD_REQUIREMENTS.md. Earlier issues are kept in the repository as JGDMS/docs/LAYOUT-proxy-host-board-2026-11-15.html and -2026-11-22.html.

NOTES

What these drawings do not settle

  • Nothing here is to scale. Card size (about 120 to 140 mm by 100 to 110 mm), card pitch (about 22 to 30 mm) and the width left for the relay duct are estimates until the SMARC connector stack heights and module heights are read from vendor drawings (open point 23).
  • The network module is a candidate, not a decision: Innodisk EGPL-G401 (K12), proposed for the prototype and production, with an I350-AM4 board as the fallback. Innodisk must confirm the LAN connectors' pinout (two ports per connector would give one lead per card), where the magnetics are, the mating connector and custom leads, supply life and the configuration memory (open point 22). The I350 is rated to 55 degrees C and its expected discontinuance is 1H 2031.
  • The relay board is a candidate: MSI D3061 (K13), about 150 W even at a fixed underclock; each network module in its own IOMMU group to be confirmed on a booted board (open point 26). It cannot boot without its DC-SCM module (MGT1), whose BMC is hardened and kept off every network but management (K15); questions for MSI are open point 31, the cable build 32, the prototype bench 33.
  • The thermal test: 35 degrees C inlet, full load, a filter partly blocked, one fan stopped at each stage in turn (open point 24).
  • The controller board, the backplane's chips and the harness (open point 28); power distribution, eFuses and pin staggering (29); updating the cards' boot flash with the lid open (30).
  • Case height: 3U is the target (operator, 2026-11-23), with the middle and rear fan heights to check; depth about 550 to 600 mm (open point 27).
  • One relay as a single point of failure for 24 modules (open point 14); one case per host or shared (12); the heartbeat, now watched by the management station (13).
