Cooling and module requirements: RL-JGDMS-0218 and -0219. NOTES

What these drawings do not settle

  • Nothing here is to scale. Card size (about 120 to 140 mm by 100 to 110 mm), card pitch (about 22 to 30 mm) and the width left for the relay duct are estimates until the SMARC connector stack heights and module heights are read from vendor drawings (open point 23).
  • The network controller is a candidate, not a decision: Innodisk EGPL-G401 (K12), with Commell M2-3504 and LR-Link LRES2030PT-4RJ45 as same-controller alternatives. How its ports reach the card connectors needs Innodisk's cable pinout or a custom daughter board (open point 22). The I350 is rated to 55 degrees C and its expected discontinuance is 1H 2031.
  • The thermal test of rear modules, network controllers and the relay duct with a fan failed (open point 24). The network card now sits at the front of the relay duct in fresh air; the test checks it and the swing in the relay CPU's intake temperature with traffic.
  • Module vendor checks: boot from the card's flash, any on-module microcontroller, variants without eMMC, five free GPIO lines (open point 25); the relay board's CPU on the duct side and a connector for the backplane cable (open point 26).
  • Case height: 3U is the target (operator, 2026-11-23). Estimated stack from the floor: standoffs, backplane, connector and a card of about 100 mm come to roughly 110 mm against about 125 mm inside 3U; fans of 80 or 92 mm; the relay's full-height cards and CPU heatsink must fit too (open point 27).
  • One relay as a single point of failure for 24 modules (open point 14); one case per host or shared (12); whose heartbeat governs the controller (13).
