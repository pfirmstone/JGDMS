NOTES

What these drawings do not settle

  • Nothing here is to scale. The bay layout, bay pitch, caddy envelope and heatsink height are estimates in the source document.
  • The position of the controller and of the managed egress switch inside the chassis is not stated anywhere; sheet 2 shows them dashed.
  • The event mailbox node and the verdict registry from the design summary are not drawn. The board document has neither yet, and adding the mailbox as a node type is a proposal (F5, F11).
  • The watchdog on the row board is the board document's W1 to W3. Proposal F19 in the design summary adds how it is fed and what the host does with resets.
  • Open in the source: the control bus between controller and row boards, the five-port switch part, which modules expose two Ethernet MACs, and the cooling margin (tests T-C1 and T-C2).
